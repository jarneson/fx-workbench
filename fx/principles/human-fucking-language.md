# Human Fucking Language

## Use when

Writing or naming anything a person must understand or act on: documentation, runbooks, plans, task packets, explanations, alert text, APIs, controls, and review text.

## Rule

Use the words people already use. Name the actual system, event, failure, action, and owner. Remove invented umbrella terms, noun piles, vague actors, stale implementation history, and qualifications that change no decision. Keep established technical terms when they are the most accurate words, and define them once when the reader may not know them. Be terse, not vague.

## Decision changed

Invent a broad label and explain it → use concrete established words that let the reader understand or act immediately.

## Proof

The intended reader can say what happened, who owns it, and what to do next without translating new jargon; simplification preserves retry behavior, failure cases, ownership, and other facts that affect a decision.
