# Do Less

## Use when

You are about to add a helper, abstraction, compatibility layer, flag, or second path.

## Rule

First try to delete, reuse, or simplify. Make the smallest coherent change that fully solves the user’s problem. Do not save lines by leaving the wrong design in place.

## Decision changed

Add another path or layer → remove or reshape what already exists.

## Proof

The result has fewer concepts or paths to understand, no duplicated decision, and still proves the real behavior.
